# Definition
When an [[Electromagnetic Waves|electromagnetic wave]] [[Reflections and Refractions|reflects]] off of a new medium, and if the wave reflects back into the same direction it came from, a standing wave will form from the interference of the incident wave with the reflected wave.

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/62/SWR-m-08.gif?utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

VSWR, or voltage standing wave ratio, is a measurement of the standing wave's maximum amplitude compared to its minimum
