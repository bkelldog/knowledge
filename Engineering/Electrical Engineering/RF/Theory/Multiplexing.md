# Definition
Multiplexing refers to any of a variety of RF techniques which allow RF signals to intelligibly exist together without interfering or losing meaningful data. Singals can be multiplexed according to space, time, frequency, amplitude, and phase. These are called spatial multiplexing, time-domain multiplexing, frequency-domain multiplexing, amplitude multiplexing, and phase multiplexing.

# Types of Multiplexing
## Spatial Multiplexing
Transmitting at different locations. Due to the natural attenuation of signal strength as it travels through space, caused by the inverse square law, all EM signals are bound within a radius in space, outside of which the signal becomes too small to practically detect.

## Time-Domain Multiplexing
Transmitting at different times.

## Frequency-Domain Multiplexing
Transmitting at different frequencies.

See [[Frequency Domain]].

## Amplitude Multiplexing
Transmitting at different amplitudes.

## Phase Multiplexing
Transmitting at different phases.
