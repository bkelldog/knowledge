# Definition
$$
e^{i \theta}=\cos(\theta)+i \sin(\theta)
$$

## Euler's Identity
A natural consequence of this formula is the following equation, derived by rearranging the terms of the formula, 
$$
e^{i \theta}=\cos(\theta)+i \sin(\theta)
$$
$$
\text{if} \>\> \theta=\pi \to e^{i\pi}=\cos(\pi)+i\sin(\pi)
$$
$$
\cos(\pi)=-1
$$
$$
\sin(\pi)=0
$$
$$
\therefore \>\>e^{i\pi}=-1 \>\> \text{or}\>\>e^{i\pi}+1=0
$$
