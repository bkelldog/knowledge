# Real Analyticity
A function $f(x)$ is analytic at a point $a$ in its [[Domain, Range, Codomain|domain]] if it satisfies the two following conditions:
1. On some open interval around $a$ in the real domain, $(b, c) \in \mathbb{R}$, $f(x)$ can be expressed as its convergent [[The Taylor Series|Taylor series]], 
$$
f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} c_{n}(x-a)^n=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n \tag{1}
$$
2. $f(x)$ is infinitely [[Differentiability|differentiable]] everywhere on its domain, meaning that it has [[The Differential|derivatives]] of every order which exist at all points on its domain.
