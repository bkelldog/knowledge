# Definition
The Fourier transform of an **aperiodic** and integrable function $g(x)$, resulting in a new function $\hat{g}(\omega)$, is given as,
$$
\hat{g}(f)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(x)\>e^{-2\pi ix f}\>dx
$$
where $f$ represents the frequency of the $e^{-2 \pi ixf}$ term, which, by [[Euler's Formula]], is of course a sinusoid. Some people (like me) prefer to rewrite this equation with $\omega=2\pi f$, to arrive at, 
$$
\hat{g}(\omega)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(x)e^{-ix\omega}dx
$$
